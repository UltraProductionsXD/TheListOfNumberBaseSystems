Base-4 | Digits: 0, 1, 2, 3

The base-4 number system, also known as the quaternary numeral system, uses four digits to represent numbers. Unlike the decimal system, base-4 is limited to four. Base-4 is closely related to binary (base-2) since each base-4 digit can be represented by two binary digits. This makes conversions between these bases straightforward.
