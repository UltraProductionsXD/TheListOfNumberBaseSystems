Silvernary Numbers From 0 - 83

Base-σ (2.4142135623...) | Digits: 0, 1, 2

Silvernary (base-σ) number representations use an irrational base, specifically the silver ratio (σ = 1 + √2), and are written using digits 0, 1, 2 — much like base-3, but with a twist due to the irrational base. And because σ satisfies the recurrence relation σ² = 2σ + 1, its powers show fractal-like or recursive structure in the digit sequences. NOTE: Base-Irrational Number Systems with whole numbers having a finite amount of digits are interesting in my opinion.
