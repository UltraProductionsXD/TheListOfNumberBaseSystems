The base-phi system is a non-integer positional numeral system that employs the golden ratio (φ) as its base. This system is sometimes referred to as phinary or golden mean base. Unlike traditional bases, such as binary or decimal, base-phi allows for the representation of numbers without using the digit sequence "11" in its standard form. This unique property arises from the algebraic characteristics of φ, where φ^n + φ^(n-1) = φ^(n+1). NOTE: This base is my favorite one out of all of them!
