Variation B

Base-5B | Digits: 0, +, -, ×, ÷

Quinary (Base-5) is a numeral system with five as the base. A possible origination of a quinary system is that there are five digits on either hand. As five is a prime number, only the reciprocals of the powers of five terminate, although its location between two highly composite numbers (4 and 6) guarantees that many recurring fractions have relatively short periods.
