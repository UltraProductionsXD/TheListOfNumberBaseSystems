The Psinary number system is a non-integer positional numeral system that uses the supergolden ratio (ψ) as its base. ψ satisfies the equation x³ = x² + 1, making ψ a Pisot number. Similar to how φ (phi, the golden ratio) satisfies φ² = φ + 1.
