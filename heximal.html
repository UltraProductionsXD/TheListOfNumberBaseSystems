Heximal Numbers From 0 - 432

Base-6 | Digits: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

A heximal numeral system (also known as base-6, senary, or seximal) has six as its base. It has been adopted independently by a small number of cultures. Like the decimal base-↊, the base is a semiprime, though it is unique as the product of the only two consecutive numbers that are both prime (2 and 3). As six is a superior highly composite number, many of the arguments made in favor of the dozenal system also apply to the heximal system.
