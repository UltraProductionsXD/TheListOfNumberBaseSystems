Base-3 | Digits: 0, 1, 2

The base-3 number system, also known as the ternary numeral system, consists of three digits: 0, 1, and 2. In a ternary number, each position from right to left represents increasing powers of three, similar to how each digit in the decimal system represents a power of ten.
